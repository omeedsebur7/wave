# WAVE Community Content Policy

**Version 1.0 — seed draft**

> As with the Seller Agreement: written to be readable and enforceable, not
> yet legally reviewed. Load into `legal_documents/content-policy` in Firestore.

WAVE is a place to sell things by showing them. These rules exist so that stays
possible.

## The short version

Post what you are selling. Do not post things that hurt people.

## What is not allowed

**Content involving minors.** No sexualised content involving anyone under 18,
in any form, real or drawn. No content that endangers a child. This is reported
to the relevant authorities, not just removed.

**Sexual content.** No pornography or sexually explicit material. Lingerie,
swimwear and similar goods may be shown as products; content whose purpose is
sexual rather than commercial is not allowed.

**Violence and harm.** No graphic violence, gore, or content promoting
self-harm, suicide or eating disorders. No threats against anyone.

**Hate.** No content attacking people on the basis of ethnicity, religion,
nationality, disability, gender, sexual orientation or similar characteristics.

**Harassment.** No targeting individuals with abuse, no coordinated pile-ons, no
posting someone's personal information.

**Deception.** No fake listings, no impersonating another person or business, no
manipulated media presented as real, no fake reviews or ratings.

**Illegal and regulated goods.** As set out in the Seller Agreement.

**Spam.** No repetitive posting, no unrelated promotional content, no automated
comment posting.

**Other people's work.** Do not post video, music, images or text you do not
have the right to use.

## Comments

Comments are open to anyone with an account, not only to people who have bought
something. That is deliberate: the most useful comment on a listing is usually a
question from someone deciding whether to buy.

The same rules apply to comments as to everything else. Rate limits apply.

## Reporting

Every Reel, product, profile, comment and message has a report option. Reports
are reviewed by a person.

Reporting is confidential — the person you report is not told who reported them.
We do not share the outcome of a report, because doing so would let someone test
what they can get away with.

## What happens when something breaks these rules

Depending on what it is and whether it has happened before: the content is
removed; the account is warned; the account loses the ability to sell, comment
or post; or the account is closed. Serious cases — particularly anything
involving children — are actioned immediately and reported to the authorities.

## If you think we got it wrong

You can respond to any enforcement decision through the app. A different
reviewer will look at it.
