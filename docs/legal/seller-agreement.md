# WAVE Seller Agreement

**Version 1.0 — seed draft**

> This is a starting draft written to be readable, not a substitute for legal
> review. Have a lawyer qualified in your operating jurisdiction check it
> before launch, particularly the liability, tax and dispute sections. Load the
> reviewed text into `legal_documents/seller-agreement` in Firestore with a
> `version` field; bumping that version triggers re-acceptance in the app.

## 1. Who this is between

This agreement is between you (the seller) and WAVE. It applies from the moment
you list an item.

WAVE is a marketplace. When someone buys from you, the sale is **between you and
that buyer**. WAVE is not the seller, does not own the goods, and is not a party
to the sale. That distinction matters for everything below.

## 2. What you are responsible for

- **The item being what you said it is.** Photos, description, condition, size
  and quantity must be accurate. A listing that misleads is grounds for removal.
- **Having the item.** Do not list what you cannot supply. Repeatedly cancelling
  orders after they are placed affects your trust tier and may end your ability
  to sell here.
- **Sending it.** Once an order is confirmed, dispatch it in the time you
  advertised. Keep the buyer informed through the app if something changes.
- **Legality.** The item must be legal to sell where you are and where the buyer
  is, and you must have the right to sell it.
- **Your own tax and licensing.** WAVE does not withhold, calculate, or file
  taxes on your behalf, and does not issue tax invoices for your sales.

## 3. What you may not sell

Anything illegal; counterfeit or replica goods; stolen goods; weapons or
ammunition; drugs, including prescription medicines; live animals; human remains
or bodily fluids; hazardous or explosive materials; anything requiring a licence
you do not hold; anything that infringes someone else's trademark or copyright;
and anything the Community Content Policy prohibits.

This list is not exhaustive. If you are unsure, ask before listing.

## 4. Ratings and your trust tier

Buyers who received an order can rate you. Those ratings, together with your
number of completed orders, determine your trust tier.

- Tiers are calculated automatically from completed transactions. They cannot be
  bought, and WAVE does not adjust them on request.
- You may not solicit, incentivise or pressure buyers for ratings, create
  accounts to rate yourself, or arrange transactions that exist only to generate
  ratings. Doing so will remove your tier and may end your account.
- A rating will only be removed if it breaches the Community Content Policy —
  not because you disagree with it.

## 5. Fees

Any commission, listing or payment-processing fee is set out separately and
shown to you before it applies. WAVE will give reasonable notice before changing
fees, and a change never applies retroactively to orders already placed.

## 6. Cancellations, returns and disputes

- A buyer may cancel before an order moves to "On the way".
- After that, returns and refunds are between you and the buyer, subject to any
  consumer rights that apply where the buyer lives — which may give them rights
  you cannot contract out of.
- WAVE may step in where there is evidence of fraud or a clear breach of this
  agreement, and may withhold a payout while investigating. WAVE is not a court
  and does not adjudicate ordinary disagreements about quality or taste.

## 7. Your content

You keep ownership of the photos, videos and text you upload. You give WAVE a
non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, display, resize and
distribute that content **for the purpose of operating and promoting the
marketplace**. That licence ends when you delete the content, except for copies
already made by buyers or held in backups for a limited period.

You confirm you have the right to grant that licence — that the photos are yours
to use, and that anyone identifiable in a video has agreed to appear.

## 8. Suspension and termination

You may stop selling at any time. WAVE may suspend or remove your ability to
sell if you breach this agreement, if there is credible evidence of fraud, or if
required by law. Where it is safe and lawful to do so, you will be told why and
be able to respond.

Outstanding orders must still be fulfilled or refunded.

## 9. Liability

WAVE provides the platform as it is. To the extent the law allows, WAVE is not
liable for lost profits or lost sales.

Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited — including for
death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

## 10. Changes

Material changes to this agreement will be notified in the app, and you will be
asked to accept the new version before continuing to sell.
